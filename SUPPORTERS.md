## How this list works

- **Only public sponsorships are listed.** GitHub lets you sponsor privately; if you do,
  you are not named here. Anonymity is the default we honor, not a favor.
- **Listing starts at $3/month** and uses whatever name or handle your GitHub profile
  shows, linked to your GitHub profile.
- **Ask for anything different and you get it** — a different name, no link, or no entry
  at all. Open an issue or reach out; no reason needed.
- **Removal on request.** Ending a sponsorship does not erase past support, so entries
  stay unless you ask otherwise.
- Updated by hand, so there may be a lag of a few days.
